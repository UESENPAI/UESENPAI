## ◈ Researcher's Note

I define the rules, distribute information, and decide what each agent stands to gain or lose.

Then I watch what happens when the same decision cannot satisfy everyone.

Give them a common goal, and they may learn to cooperate. Give them a private incentive, and the experiment becomes more interesting.

I study **autonomous agents that exercise judgment, cooperate across conflicting interests, and support appropriate human trust**.

That includes the inconvenient cases: an agent that questions the task, a team that negotiates its own terms, or a system that has good reasons to refuse.

I keep those logs.

## ◈ Standard Procedure

```text
01. Define the task, stakeholders, and conflicting goals
02. Construct a controlled virtual environment
03. Set objectives, information, incentives, and safety constraints
04. Train agents. Introduce uncertainty
05. Observe cooperation, negotiation, refusal, and adaptation
06. Test performance, safety, robustness, and generalization
07. Study human–agent interaction and trust calibration
08. Record the findings. Revise the system. Begin again
```
